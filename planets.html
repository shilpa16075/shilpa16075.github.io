☀️ The Sun

The Sun is the heart of our solar system. It provides light, heat, and energy to sustain life on Earth. Composed mostly of hydrogen and helium, it is a giant ball of plasma that generates immense energy through nuclear fusion.

☿ Mercury

Mercury is the smallest and closest planet to the Sun. It has no atmosphere to trap heat, causing extreme temperature differences between day and night.

♀ Venus

Venus, often called Earth's twin, is covered with thick clouds of sulfuric acid. Its surface temperature is hot enough to melt lead due to the greenhouse effect.

🌍 Earth

Earth is the only known planet that supports life. It has water, an oxygen-rich atmosphere, and a balanced climate system that sustains millions of species.

♂ Mars

Known as the Red Planet, Mars has the largest volcano and canyon in the solar system. Scientists believe it may have once had water on its surface.

♃ Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system. It’s a gas giant with a massive storm called the Great Red Spot that has raged for centuries.

♄ Saturn

Saturn is known for its stunning ring system made of ice and rock particles. It is the second-largest planet and has many moons.

♅ Uranus

Uranus rotates on its side, making its seasons extremely unusual. Its blue color is due to methane in its atmosphere.

♆ Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun. It is a deep blue gas giant with strong winds and massive storms.
