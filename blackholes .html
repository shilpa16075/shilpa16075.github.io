🌑 Sagittarius A*

At the heart of our Milky Way lies a monster — Sagittarius A*, a supermassive black hole with a mass of over 4 million Suns. Hidden behind clouds of dust and gas, it silently consumes matter while bending light itself around its core. It’s the beating dark heart of our galaxy.

⚫ Cygnus X-1

Cygnus X-1 was the first black hole ever discovered and remains one of the most powerful X-ray sources in the night sky. Formed from a collapsed massive star, it continuously pulls matter from a nearby blue supergiant, creating violent X-ray outbursts visible across space.

🌘 Messier 87* (M87*)

This giant black hole in the center of the Virgo A galaxy is so massive that its event horizon could engulf our entire solar system. M87* became world-famous in 2019 when astronomers captured its first-ever real image — a glowing orange ring surrounding an infinite shadow.

🌒 TON 618

TON 618 is the largest and most luminous black hole ever known — an unimaginable cosmic giant with a mass more than 66 billion times that of the Sun. Its energy output is so vast that it outshines entire galaxies, a blazing beacon in the farthest reaches of the universe.
